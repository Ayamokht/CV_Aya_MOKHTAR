About Me

From Statistical Modeling to Intelligent Data Solutions

I graduated from the Master MoSEF in Statistical, Economic and Financial Modeling at Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University. My academic background combines econometrics, statistics and machine learning, with a strong focus on quantitative analysis and predictive modeling.

Through my professional experiences at Crédit Mutuel Factoring, METRO France and a B2B startup, I have worked on credit risk scoring, customer segmentation and data enrichment. These projects allowed me to contribute to different stages of the data lifecycle, from preparation and analysis to modeling and business-oriented solutions.

Alongside my experience in traditional data science, I am developing my skills in generative AI, including large language models, retrieval-augmented generation and AI-powered applications.
